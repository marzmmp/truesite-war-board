Research Brief — June 2026
How close are we to an artificial functioning brain?
The human brain has 100 billion neurons and 100 trillion synaptic connections. Current neuromorphic computers (Intel Loihi, IBM NorthPole) have ~1 billion neurons and ~100 billion synapses — 1/1,000th of the human brain's complexity. LANL scientists are building a system within a 2m² box targeting full cerebral cortex neuron count, operating 250,000–1,000,000× faster than biological brain, using only 10kW.

Timeline estimate: Full brain-equivalent neuromorphic hardware ~2035–2040. Software-equivalent artificial general reasoning: competitive approaches emerging now (2026). ADAMA is a software-first artificial brain — no neuromorphic hardware required to start.
